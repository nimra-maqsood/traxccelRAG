## Company Story & Timeline

Our **story begins** with envisioning a modern services solutions firm

*   **2021:** envisioned to modernize service delivery to address client business problems. Traxccel was founded in Houston, Texas.

*   **2022:** opened first offshore delivery hub in Pakistan and won our first client engagement in the manufacturing sector.

*   **2023:** expanded data enablement services portfolio, established delivery capabilities in LATAM and India through strategic partnerships.

*   **2024:** launched our flagship offering, axlFOUNDRY with a core focus on Databricks & Azure technologies.

*   **2025:** launched ReImagine2.0 offering and axlACADEMY expanded our delivery centers in Pakistan and 4x our team of professionals.

*   **2026:** looking forward – invest in our service offerings, AI-powered solutions, and capabilities in Databricks, NVIDIA, Cognite and other platforms.
