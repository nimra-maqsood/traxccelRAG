# Industries We Serve

## Industry: Oil & Gas

Data and AI optimize exploration, production, safety, and sustainability in the oil & gas industry, driving efficiency and innovation.

**Services in Oil & Gas Indsutry:**

*   **Exploration:** Use Data + AI to find and rank the best subsurface prospects faster and with higher confidence.

*   **Drilling & completion:** Apply real-time analytics to drill safer, cheaper, and complete wells with higher production.

*   **Land & lease:** Automate lease, title, and royalty workflows to protect acreage, prevent leakage, and cut cycle time.

*   **Health & safety:** Optimize pipeline and logistics operations to boost throughput, reduce losses, and enhance safety.


## Industry: Manufacturing & Distribution

Data and AI optimize production, quality control, predictive maintenance, supply chain, and workforce management, fostering efficiency and innovation.

**Services in Manufacturing & Distribution:**

*   **Fragmented & siloed data:** Critical data is scattered across systems, making it hard to trace issues, perform root-cause analysis, or close the loop to engineering.

*   **Asset uptime:** Unplanned failures and service cause downtime, expensive truck rolls, and unhappy customers due to limited foresight into health and parts needs.

*   **Warranty administration:** Warranty claims can be costly and slow to resolve due to manual reviews, incomplete data, and fraud or "no-fault-found" scenarios.

*   **Inventory management:** Misaligned demand, excess/obsolete parts, and underutilized assets create working-capital drag and missed revenue opportunities.


## Industry: Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC)

Data and AI enhance project visibility, cost control, and safety in EPC operations, ensuring efficiency, compliance, and timely delivery.

**Services in Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC):**

*   **Code & compliance:** Model checks and validation to catch design mistakes early, ensuring compliance with ASME/API/ISO codes before fabrication.

*   **Quality control & rework:** Computer vision and digital QA detect issues during fabrication and construction, eliminating costly rework and change orders.

*   **Cost & schedule overrun:** forecasting turns unreliable schedules into predictable project outcomes, cutting delays and cost blowouts.

*   **Procurement inefficiencies:** Predictive analytics and intelligent agent-based procurement keep material flow on track by forecasting shortages and automating purchase actions.
