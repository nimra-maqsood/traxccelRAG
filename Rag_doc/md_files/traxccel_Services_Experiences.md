## Relevant Experiences and Case Study for "Imagine" Service

### Experience: Data & analytics strategy

*   **Industry(s):** Energy & production

*   **Business Function(s):** Reservoir & production engineering

*   **Capability(s):** Data + AI Strategy

*   **Description:** Developed & executed a data process optimization framework to assess and optimize the subsurface landscape.

*   **Situation:** Due to inorganic growth, operations were not aligned and data ownership across groups were not clearly defined – leading to redundancy. Also, the data handling systems and procedures were not suited to economies of scale for shale operations.

*   **Solution:** With a focus on Reservoir Engineering, Well Planning, Well Execution, Well Data Management and Spatial Data Management – our team documented the as-is value streams, identified gaps, and opportunities to leverage data, AI to digitize and optimize value streams.

*   **Outcomes:**
    *   Digitized and integrated manual processes.
    *   Improved approach to higher data quality.
    *   Ingestion and processing of unstructured data.


## Relevant Experiences and Case Study for "Enable" Service

### Experience: Parts master data management

*   **Industry(s):** Manufacturing

*   **Business Function(s):** Procurement

*    **Capability(s):** Data Foundation

*   **Description:** Cleansed and enriched Parts data for improved revenue and expense allocations, spend analytics, and inventory management​.

*   **Situation:** The client realigned P&L by Product Line that highlighted issues with duplicate Parts data, taxonomy & classification. Business impacts included lack of spend visibility, inefficient strategic sourcing, poor inventory management, lost revenue, and long lead times.​

*   **Solution:** Our team analyzed 500K+ parts to rationalize, standardize, and classify ~165K priority parts in the EPICOR ERP system​. Further, our team defined standard Parts master data process and governance for proactive data quality with minimal business impact.

*   **Outcomes and Impact:**
    *   33% Reduction in duplicate parts.
    *   165,000 Standardized and enriched engineered & non-engineered parts.
    *   Improved search and inventory rationalization.
